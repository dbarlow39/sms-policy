My Starter Profile

SMS Privacy Policy · Effective date: August 18, 2026

My Starter Profile is the registered A2P 10DLC brand of Dave Barlow, a sole proprietor, and is the business responsible for this messaging program. The messaging program operated under this brand is known as Barlow Market Alerts. This policy describes how My Starter Profile handles mobile phone numbers and text message data in connection with this automated SMS notification service.

Scope of this service

This is a personal, single-recipient notification service. A command-line tool that I built and run on my own computer sends automated informational text messages to my own mobile phone number. There are no other subscribers, no mailing list, and no third-party recipients.

Information collected

The only personal information involved is a single mobile phone number — my own — which I entered into a local configuration file on my own machine. No phone numbers are collected from any other person. No names, addresses, payment details, or location data are collected in connection with this messaging service.

Mobile information is never shared. No mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in consent, or text messaging originator opt-in data are sold, rented, shared, or otherwise disclosed to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. This information is not shared with any third party for any purpose other than the delivery of the messages themselves.

Service providers

Messages are delivered through Twilio, a licensed communications provider, acting solely as the transmission carrier for the messages. Twilio processes the destination number only as necessary to deliver the message. No other party receives this information.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on how often the tool is run and what it finds. Typical volume is approximately 1–10 messages per day. Frequency may be higher or lower on any given day, and may be zero.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply. Any charges are those set by the recipient's mobile carrier plan.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance. Because I am the sole recipient, sending can also be disabled directly in the tool's configuration at any time.

Data retention

Message logs are retained by the delivery provider under its own retention schedule and are used only for troubleshooting delivery. The configuration file containing the destination number resides on my own computer and can be deleted at any time.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to dbarlow@barlow.com.
