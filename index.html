What you will receive

  • Daily market close summaries (S&P 500, Nasdaq, Dow)
  • Price and volume alerts for tickers on your watchlist
  • Financial headlines from published sources such as CNBC and Fox Business
  • Economic calendar notices ahead of scheduled data releases

Message frequency

Frequency varies and depends on market activity. Typical volume is approximately 1–10 messages per day, and may be zero on some days.

Cost

There is no charge for the program itself. Message and data rates may apply under your mobile carrier plan.

How to stop

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe immediately. You will receive one confirmation message and nothing further. Reply HELP for assistance, or email dbarlow@barlow.com.

Privacy

Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Full details are in the SMS Privacy Policy.
