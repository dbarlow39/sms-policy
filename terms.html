The service

My Starter Profile is the registered A2P 10DLC brand of Dave Barlow, a sole proprietor. Under this brand, My Starter Profile operates an automated SMS notification program known as Barlow Market Alerts. A command-line tool sends automated informational text messages containing publicly available financial market headlines and price information. The service has exactly one recipient: the operator's own mobile phone number.

Not financial advice. Messages are informational only. Nothing sent through this service constitutes investment advice, a recommendation, a solicitation, or an offer to buy or sell any security or financial instrument. Market data may be delayed or inaccurate and should not be relied upon for trading decisions.

Consent

Consent to receive these messages was given by the operator entering their own mobile number into the tool's local configuration. No consent is collected from, or on behalf of, any other person, and no messages are sent to anyone else.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on how often the tool runs. Typical volume is approximately 1–10 messages per day, and may be zero on some days.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. A single confirmation message will be sent, after which no further messages will be delivered. Sending can also be disabled directly in the tool's configuration.
